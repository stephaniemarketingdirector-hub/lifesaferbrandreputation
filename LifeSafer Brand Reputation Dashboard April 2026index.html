What's Working
Service Is Our Competitive Edge and the Data Proves It
Across every source we track, customer service and support is the area where we clearly separate from the competition.
  • Copilot ranks us #1 for friendly service (4/5 on support, Intoxalock gets 2/5)
  • Profound AI top positive themes: transparent, affordable, competitive pricing
  • NPS promoters: smooth installation, helpful support, convenient scheduling
  • TrustPilot #1 driver of 5 star reviews: customer service
  • Reddit and Facebook: unprompted recommendations and documented switches to us
Intoxalock's State Agency Issues Are Keeping the Door Open
Illinois and Florida Secretaries of State are still not fully doing business with Intoxalock following the cyberattack. This is well known in the industry. What matters for us is how we're positioned.
  • We're on approved vendor lists in these states
  • Our service reputation gives partners a reason to refer us
  • Drivers who need to switch are already finding us on their own
  • Opportunity across the business to lead with our customer experience story in these markets
